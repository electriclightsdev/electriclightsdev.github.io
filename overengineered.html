A P.S.A. ON PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT

While we are here flexing, remember: With great power comes great responsibility.

  • Accessibility first: That cool toggle switch above? It uses an actual <input type="checkbox"> under the hood so screen readers know what it is. Don't use a <div> as a button.
  • Respect the vestibular system: See that spinning cube? If someone has their OS set to reduce motion due to vestibular disorders, the CSS in this site intercepts that via @media (prefers-reduced-motion) and instantly freezes the animations. Be kind to your users.
  • Graceful degradation: If a browser doesn't support backdrop-filter, it just ignores it and shows a slightly darker box. The site still works. That's the magic of CSS.
